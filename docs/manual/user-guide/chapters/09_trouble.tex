\chapter{Troubleshooting}\label{ch:trouble}

Start with the \ui{Status} page: its red and yellow rows already say what is wrong, and the \raisebox{-1pt}{\textbf{(?)}} icon next to a
row explains the fix. The tables below collect the most common situations by where you meet them.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\section{Connecting}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.27\linewidth}L{0.30\linewidth}L{0.35\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
What you see & Likely cause & What to do\\ \midrule\endhead
\ui{Find Shake\ldots} finds nothing & the Shake is still starting; no cable; no 169.254.x.x address on the laptop & wait three to five minutes; check the cable; open \ui{Status} and use \ui{Set IP}; then try again, or add the Shake by hand with its address\\
\ui{Laptop IP} red & the wired port has no link-local address & press \ui{Set IP} (Chapter~\ref{ch:status}); check the cable and that the Shake has power\\
\ui{SeedLink} red, \emph{connection refused} & the Shake is not ready, or the address is wrong & wait; check the address in \ui{Settings}; check the cable\\
\ui{SSH} red: wrong user or password & credentials differ from the Shake's & enter the correct ones in \ui{Settings} and press \ui{Test connection}\\
\ui{SSH} red: host key changed & the Shake's SD card was replaced or re-installed & delete its line from \file{known\_hosts} in the data folder (Section~\ref{sec:folder}) and refresh\\
\ui{Clock} yellow or red & the Shake has no Internet or no time source & fix \ui{Internet} and \ui{Wi-Fi} first; give it a few minutes to synchronise\\
\ui{Wi-Fi} yellow, two addresses & two network managers on the Shake & see the \ui{Guide} page, ``Wi-Fi: two addresses''\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\section{Live}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.27\linewidth}L{0.30\linewidth}L{0.35\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
What you see & Likely cause & What to do\\ \midrule\endhead
\ui{reconnecting\ldots} with a reason & the link dropped; the page retries by itself & check the cable and the Shake's power; it resumes when the Shake is back\\
\ui{waiting for data\ldots} & connected but no data for five seconds & wait; if it persists, restart the Shake\\
Flat lines & the filter band is wrong, or the Shake is quiet & switch \ui{Filter} off to see the raw signal; tap the table or stamp near the Shake to test\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\section{Data and helicorder}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.27\linewidth}L{0.30\linewidth}L{0.35\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
What you see & Likely cause & What to do\\ \midrule\endhead
\emph{No data in this range} & nothing is on the laptop for that time & check the coverage bar: grey means ``on the Shake, not yet downloaded'': press \ui{Download \& preview}; red means nothing was recorded\\
\emph{No data yet. Connect a Shake, or import a miniSEED file.} & the data folder is empty & download from the Shake, or import a file\\
Helicorder empty for a day & that day is not on the laptop & download it on the \ui{Data} page\\
\emph{No data in this window} (detail view) & you clicked a row or time with no samples & click another row, or use \ui{Earlier}/\ui{Later}\\
Error when choosing \ui{m/s} & no instrument response and no Internet & connect the laptop to the Internet once so the response can be downloaded and cached\\
Preview is slow & a very long range, or \ui{m/s} on a whole day & select a shorter range, or use the helicorder for overviews\\
Gaps in the data & the Shake was off, or the cable was unplugged & see \ui{Data health} for exactly when; gaps cannot be filled\\
Spectrogram blank in places & the window touches a gap & expected: such columns are left blank on purpose\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\section{Starting the application}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.27\linewidth}L{0.30\linewidth}L{0.35\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
What you see & Likely cause & What to do\\ \midrule\endhead
\emph{address already in use} & you passed \code{--port} and another program uses it & start without \code{--port} (a free port is chosen) or pick another number\\
The browser does not open & started with \code{--no-browser}, or no default browser & open the address printed in the terminal by hand\\
\emph{command not found: uv} & \code{uv} is not installed & install it from \url{https://docs.astral.sh/uv/}\\
Page does not update after upgrading & an old browser tab is open & reload the page (\key{Ctrl}+\key{R}); restart the application\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Still stuck?}
Look in the \file{logs/} folder of the data folder, and read the \ui{Guide} page of the application. When reporting a problem, include
the text of any error message, the page you were on, and what the \ui{Status} page shows.
